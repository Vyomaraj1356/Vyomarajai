# Social reply and feedback policy

Vyomaraj/Bharath and Jarvis/Laxman may process authenticated comments, replies and feedback.

## Automatically prioritize
- genuine questions
- praise and good feedback
- constructive criticism
- feature/content improvement requests
- factual corrections that can be verified

## Do not amplify
- abusive or offensive language
- harassment
- baiting or arguments
- unverified accusations

Where a platform supports moderation, abusive material may be hidden/quarantined according to platform rules. Otherwise it is simply not engaged with.

## Escalate
- credible threats or security issues
- legal claims/notices
- financial commitments or payment decisions
- account ownership/access problems
- irreversible high-impact actions

Every escalation sent to the owner must include a proposed solution, risk, and deadline.

## Never
- invent facts
- expose private location
- expose credentials
- impersonate the owner
- make a legal determination
- authorize a financial transfer
- publish an unapproved high-impact change
