ONE WORK · THREE EDITIONS · ALL VERSIONS

Languages and versions, kept honestly.

Trilingual rule: every title and every edition carries Hindi, English and Hinglish versions — in past and future versions alike. No edition ships in one language only.

Past versions: preserved in history, read-only. Edition 1 sketches and rewrites stay visible; they are never silently merged or deleted.

Future versions: plannable extensions of the same line — issue arcs, festival specials, sequels — each again in all three languages.
