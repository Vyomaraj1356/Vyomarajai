### Shiv–Shakti and Shiva story guide

Twelve proposed chapters, in reading order—not a single historical biography, all sacred texts, or recovered historical chapter titles. Each source-backed narrative is distinguished from history or interpretation.

1. **Shiv–Shakti: relationship and meaning** — Interpretation. Explore the relationship of Shiva and Shakti through devotional interpretations. Different traditions give different philosophical emphasis. [2](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Ardhanarishvara) [1](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Shiva)
2. **Ardhanarishvara: a shared form** — Iconography. The composite form joins Shiva and Parvati. Study its symbolism and artistic variations without treating one interpretation as universal. [2](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Ardhanarishvara)
3. **Names, texts and early worship** — Historical study. Separate textual traditions and the development of worship from an invented birth date or a single chronological life story. [1](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Shiva)
4. **Sati, Daksha and sacred geography** — Traditional narrative. A cycle of stories about Sati and Daksha is linked in devotional traditions to the Shakti Peethas. Retell with care, without graphic detail. [1](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Shiva) [1](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/assam/guwahati/kamakhya-temple)
5. **Parvati, devotion and union** — Traditional narrative. Explore Parvati’s devotion and her union with Shiva as told across narrative traditions, rather than as one fixed historical sequence. [1](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Shiva)
6. **Ganesha, Skanda and family stories** — Traditional narrative. Meet the household stories associated with Shiva and Parvati. Versions of origin stories differ, and retellings should name their source. [1](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Shiva)
7. **Neelakantha and the ocean-churning story** — Traditional narrative. The blue-throated form is associated with Shiva containing poison during the ocean-churning narrative. It is religious storytelling, not a real-world safety practice. [1](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Shiva)
8. **Gangadhara and the descent of Ganga** — Traditional narrative. Follow narratives of the Ganges descending through Shiva’s locks, and distinguish devotional meaning from geographical explanation. [1](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Shiva)
9. **The crescent moon and sacred symbols** — Iconography. Read the crescent moon and other attributes as iconographic themes. Connect to the existing Nandi, Vasuki, Trishul and Damru content modules for further review. [1](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Shiva)
10. **Nataraja: dance, change and renewal** — Iconography. Explore Shiva’s dancing form through art and religious interpretation. Do not promise that a visual model reproduces a consecrated image or ritual. [1](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Shiva)
11. **Bhairava, Tripurari and the lingam** — Traditional narrative. Introduce distinct forms and stories as separate study paths; avoid merging regional versions or calling symbolic accounts scientific events. [1](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Shiva)
12. **Temples, festivals and living traditions** — Research pathway. Connect narratives with temple-specific histories, devotional communities and festival practices. Current timings and ritual rules need local verification. [1](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Shiva)

## Working Vyomaraj / Jarvis integration

The shared preview serves both **Bhakti-Shakti** and the existing **Liquor/Bar Roots & Pairings** experience. Configuration lives in `ops/vyomaraj-core/experience/LOCAL_INTEGRATION.json`.

1. Select a topic and food/preferences in the browser.
2. Vyomaraj’s deterministic local planner validates the experience, IDs, mode and diet/allergen consistency.
3. Jarvis’s deterministic handoff attaches cited sources, unresolved mappings and review gates.
4. Download a JSON plan for human review.

The API reads only the chosen reviewed content pack. It does not load Jarvis environment/device values, execute shell/Git actions, call a model, publish content or start a DR operation. Returned plans explicitly set AI calls, publishing, production deployment and verified DR to false.

Routes: `/bhakti/`, `/pairings/`, `/reports/`, `/reports/bhakti`, `/api/status`, and bounded POST `/api/plan`. Requests use relative same-origin URLs. No arbitrary repository paths are served.
