# Sovereign — Entertainment, Choice & Respect

**Owner-directed platform policy · 3 October 2026**

Vyomaraj is intended for **entertainment, cultural viewing and lawful creator earning opportunities**. Spiritual, religious and traditional-practice material is **view-only context**, not a direction to perform it. Viewing is voluntary. Vyomaraj does not require anyone to follow a belief, teacher, ritual, remedy or practice.

## Dignity and non-harm

- Respect humans, animals, religious and cultural sentiments, religions, castes, creeds, communities, belief and non-belief. No humiliation, discrimination, incitement, cruelty, coercion or exploitation.
- Do not supply hazardous ritual procedures or present ritual, mantra, ashes, blessing or spiritual upayas as cures. No clinical diagnosis, prescription or promised supernatural result.
- Do not pressure anyone to imitate depictions, disclose private beliefs, pay for claimed protection, abandon healthcare or participate in a ceremony.
- Separate theological narratives, historical evidence, institutional claims and fictional/screen adaptations. New editorial chapter mappings remain proposals; no spiritual-power rankings.
- General safety information remains visible, including seeking qualified care and India's emergency number 112. A religious narrative does not replace medical care.
- Respectful discussion and differing beliefs are permitted. A commitment to care is not a promise that nobody can ever be offended. Reports of harm or infringement need real human review, not a disclaimer alone.

## What is enforced in this viewer

Aghor plan creation and downloadable practice plans are removed. The API rejects Aghor planning requests, including the previously available study/reflection/service modes. All six practice cards are contextual descriptions with **no operational steps**. Source links, filters, timelines, profiles and general care boundaries remain available for viewing.

Other creative tools—such as consent-based local media editing and food visualization—remain separate voluntary features. They do not authorize religious instruction, unsafe conduct, publication without rights or real-world execution. This is not a blanket claim that every endpoint in the application is read-only.

These rules add a platform-governance layer; they do not move Bhakti-Shakti into the counted Entertainment category or change the 128-slot registry. Historical sovereign-role declarations are preserved as source history, not treated as legal powers, regulators or security guarantees.

## Earning without pressure or false promises

Creator earnings may be possible only where content rights, consent, age restrictions, applicable laws, platform rules and commercial disclosures permit them. There are **no guaranteed earnings**, no connected payout/payment service and no approval to monetize another person's content, identity or sacred material without the necessary permissions.

The Contracts tab contains draft participation/creator terms for review. Opening a page does not execute a contract or collect acceptance.

## Scope and limitations

This is an owner-approved product policy, not legal advice, clinical advice, legal-compliance certification or a guarantee of zero harm. Production moderation, accessible complaint handling, participant consent records, enforceable agreements and payments require separate implementation and review before a public earning service launches.
