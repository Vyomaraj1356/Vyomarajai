## Important distinctions retained

- **Work vs production:** a playwright’s text, a translation, a particular staging and a film adaptation are separate. The NSD Ek Zunj Varyashi listing describes a 150-minute production; this is not every staging’s runtime. [1](https://www.21brm.nsd.gov.in/category/all-plays/page/2/)
- **Old text vs new performance:** NSD’s Adhe Adhure record is for 13 February 2026 in Mumbai, already in the past. It does not mean Mohan Rakesh’s text was newly written in 2026. [2](https://www.brm.nsd.gov.in/event/adhe-adhure/)
- **Natsamrat:** the selected ZEE5 record lists a 2019 version with credits different from the familiar 2016 film. Its catalogue/recorded-stage classification needs confirmation before merging records. [1](https://www.zee5.com/global/movies/details/natsamrat/0-0-101198)
- **Cadbury original year:** ET BrandEquity cites 1993, whereas Exchange4media cites 1994. The prototype leaves the original year unresolved; the 2021 reimagining is a distinct case study. [2](https://brandequity.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/advertising/cadburys-iconic-cricket-ad-gets-a-2021-twist/86284165) [3](https://www.exchange4media.com/advertising-news/hall-of-ads-asli-swad-zindagi-ka-the-ad-that-changed-how-india-celebrated-forever-147392.html)
- **Open licensing is specific:** Sintel’s project page requires the entire credit scroll when screening the movie and attribution for other reuse, with logo/trademark exclusions. The Library of Congress also calls for item-specific and territory-aware rights assessment. [2](https://durian.blender.org/sharing/) [1](https://www.loc.gov/collections/national-screening-room/about-this-collection/rights-and-access/)
- **Ads need evidence and disclosures:** ASCI guidance provides a review reference, not automatic approval of an ad or its claims. [1](https://www.ascionline.in/the-asci-code-guidelines/)

## Working original-production planner

Seven formats: **feature film, short film, clip/trailer, long play, one-act/short play, original advert and mixed editorial sequence**. Each creates contiguous timing budgets and production beats through the shared POST `/api/plan` endpoint.

Two original seeds—**The Shared Shelf** and **The Repair Table**—include short Marathi, Hindi and English dialogue samples. Planning notes are in English. This is a template-based local function, not a model call, a complete script, or adaptation of selected commercial references. Feature/long-play plans require substantial writing and rehearsal before production.

Vyomaraj validates selections and routes existing slots. Jarvis attaches sources, timing budgets and review gates. Plans download as JSON and explicitly report no AI calls, rendering, streaming, publishing, production deployment or DR verification.

## Working local cut / mix preview

- Accept up to six browser-supported videos: 100 MB per file and 300 MB combined, with finite readable duration.
- Require a user acknowledgement of rights or another lawful basis. This is not automated legal clearance.
- Set valid in/out points, reorder or remove clips and preview the sequence with hard cuts and each clip’s original sound.
- Export an **edit-decision JSON**, including filenames and timing—not video data or an encoded MP4.
- Files remain in this browser tab. No media or filenames are sent to the planning API; there is no upload endpoint.
- No autoplay on selecting files. Clearing files or revoking the acknowledgement stops playback and releases local object URLs.
- Preview timing is approximate, not frame-accurate. No transitions, soundtrack mixing, captions, remote URL import or production rendering are implemented.

The user can therefore plan new films/plays/ads and assemble a local preview sequence from authorized footage. The prototype does **not** supply all requested full movies as playable hosted copies or render a finished film/ad automatically.
