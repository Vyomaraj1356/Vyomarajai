# Contracts — Entertainment & Creator Participation

**DRAFT FOR REVIEW · not signed, not executed, no acceptance collected.**

These proposed terms reflect the owner's non-harm and voluntary-participation policy. They do not overwrite historical source contracts, bind a viewer by visiting this page, waive statutory rights or constitute legal advice. Appropriate legal review is required before commercial use.

## 1. Purpose and voluntary choice

Vyomaraj provides entertainment, cultural viewing and potential lawful creator earning opportunities. Spiritual/traditional material is contextual and view-only. Nobody is required to follow, perform, imitate, believe in or pay for a ritual, teaching or remedy. Leaving or declining participation must not attract threats, discrimination or pressure.

## 2. Safety, respect and dignity

Participants must not use the platform to harm humans or animals, promote cruelty, exploit vulnerability, coerce belief or demean people because of religion, caste, creed, community or other identity. Treat religious and cultural sentiments respectfully while permitting good-faith discussion. No hazardous ritual instructions or claims of ritual cures; no instruction to stop necessary professional care.

The entertainment label does not make dangerous or unlawful conduct acceptable. A disclaimer does not eliminate duties to protect people or applicable legal rights.

## 3. Rights, consent and privacy

Creators need the rights and permissions appropriate to their content, music, footage, text, likenesses and intended commercial use. Credit alone is not a licence. Obtain relevant participant consent and additional safeguards for minors. No unauthorized publication of private information, recordings or images; a viewer's local media stays local unless a separately reviewed upload/publication flow is introduced.

## 4. Earnings and commercial disclosures

Earnings are opportunities, not promises. No minimum income, payout date, commission percentage, revenue share or payment provider is configured by these draft terms. Before a real commercial arrangement, disclose parties, licensed rights, deliverables, duration, cancellation/refund arrangements, fees, tax responsibilities, payout conditions and dispute handling in a separately agreed contract. Disclose sponsorships and comply with applicable platform and advertising rules.

No payment or legal acceptance is collected here. Viewing content or exporting metadata does not create an earning entitlement or licence to redistribute source works.

## 5. Complaints, moderation and withdrawal

Before public commercial launch, designate a working contact and complaint/appeal procedure, responsibilities for investigating harm and infringement, reasonable removal/correction processes and retention/privacy rules. This preview does not pretend that a staffed complaint service or automated moderation system exists. Immediate danger should be directed to local emergency services; in India call 112.

## 6. Review gates before activation

- Identify the actual contracting parties and authorized signatories.
- Review jurisdiction, consumer protection, privacy, IP, advertising, age restrictions, accessibility, taxes and other applicable requirements with qualified advisers.
- Configure explicit informed acceptance, a versioned terms record and accessible copies; do not assume acceptance from a page visit.
- Test payments, refunds, disputes and consent withdrawal before enabling a commercial flow.
- Preserve platform non-harm rules. No draft provision should be read as overriding mandatory rights or authorizing unsafe content.
