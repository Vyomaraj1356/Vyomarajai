### Radio nostalgia and the “Bianaca” request

The likely intended show is **Binaca Geetmala**. The cited history links it to Ameen Sayani and Radio Ceylon (1952–1988), followed by Vividh Bharati (1989–1994). The prototype keeps this as a historical discovery card—not a recovered programme archive. [3](https://indianexpress.com/article/explained/explained-culture/100-years-of-radio-ceylon-history-significance-10435302/)

Prasar Bharati lists Bhoole Bisre Geet, Chhayageet, Sangeet Sarita, Jaimala and Hawamahal among its programmes. These give several nostalgia and spoken-audio routes without pretending they all use the same countdown format. Current airtimes and playable episodes are not imported. [1](https://prasarbharati.gov.in/en/vividh-bharati/)

The UK chart-radio reference comes from Official Charts’ description of its BBC Radio 1 unveiling. Other international radio dossiers, including American Top 40/Casey Kasem and Pick of the Pops, remain explicit research backlog rather than invented completed archives. [1](https://www.officialcharts.com/about/)

### Charts and top-list formats

The directory distinguishes Billboard Hot 100, Billboard 200, Global 200, India Songs, Official UK charts, Spotify, YouTube and an annual IFPI snapshot. Publisher, market, time period and ranking unit must remain together. Billboard Global 200 uses streaming and sales across more than 200 territories; Spotify chart-eligible streams differ from raw app counts; YouTube song/video/Shorts measures are distinct. [1](https://www.billboard.com/charts/hot-100/) [1](https://www.billboard.com/charts/billboard-global-200/) [1](https://support.spotify.com/us/artists/article/understanding-spotify-charts/) [1](https://support.google.com/youtubemusic/answer/9014376?hl=en)

## Working integration

- `/music/`: search by artist/title/language; filter collection, era and region.
- Queue up to eight cards, reorder/remove them, and select radio/audio/video plus a 15/30/60-minute planning budget.
- Vyomaraj validates and routes to the proposed functions of existing ENT-MUS slots.
- Jarvis prepares a contiguous timed editorial rundown with sources, credits, rights gates and downloadable JSON.
- Timings are **slot budgets**, not verified track durations, a rendered episode or a third-party playlist.
- Local audio/video files can be previewed in the browser after permission acknowledgement. Files are not uploaded; nothing autoplays. Unsupported codecs produce a visible error.
- Bhakti and Liquor/Bar link to Music; the shared report viewer includes this report.
