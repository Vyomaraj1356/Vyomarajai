DIGNITY IS COMPATIBLE WITH MEDICAL CARE

Upayas are not diagnoses.

Do not substitute ritual for healthcare. No ash, bathing ritual, mantra or blessing is presented as a cure. Keep prescribed care intact. Serious symptoms or immediate danger require urgent professional help; India’s emergency number is 112.
