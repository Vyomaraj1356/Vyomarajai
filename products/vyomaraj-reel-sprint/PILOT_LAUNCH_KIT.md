# Vyomaraj Reel Sprint — customer validation kit

**Internal launch prep — 7 October 2026.** This kit prepares a small customer-validation test. No prospect has been contacted, no price has been approved, and no revenue is claimed.

## 1. Offer to test

**For:** independent cafés and small hospitality venues in Pune (first-segment hypothesis, not validated market research).

**Pilot deliverable:** four original 20–30 second content briefs with hooks, voiceover/on-screen copy, shot prompts, captions and CTA; a one-week publishing calendar; and a source/rights/claim checklist. One revision after the client reviews the draft.

**Not included:** filming, editing, paid ads, account access, automatic publishing, platform growth or sales promises, performance guarantees, or reuse of unlicensed footage/music/likenesses.

**Suggested test fee:** choose one price between **₹2,500 and ₹5,000** only after the owner approves it. This is a pricing experiment, not a market benchmark. Confirm scope, due date, revision limit, cancellation/refund terms, rights, tax/invoice handling and payment method before accepting money. No checkout is connected to the prototype.

## 2. Demo policy

Use the **Load sample** button in the app to generate a sample for “Vyomaraj Demo Café.” It is a fictional example. Do not present it as a real café, customer, endorsement, case study or business result. Before showing a real customer's name, logo, menu, team or metrics, get permission and verify the material they provide.

## 3. Copy to personalize before sending

### Short English message

> Hi [Name] — I’m testing a small content-planning service for independent cafés in Pune. The pilot is four original short-video concepts/scripts with shot prompts, captions, a one-week calendar and a review checklist. It doesn’t include filming or posting, and I don’t promise views or sales. I have a clearly fictional sample I can show you. Would you be open to a 10-minute feedback chat? If the scope is useful, I can share a paid-pilot quote after I understand what you need.

**Personalize every message.** Use a public business contact channel or a warm introduction, ask permission to share the sample, do not bulk-spam, and honor a “no” or opt-out. These drafts have not been sent.

## 4. Ten-minute discovery questions

1. How do you currently plan Reels/Shorts, and who does the work?
2. What part takes the most time or causes the most rework?
3. Which action matters most this month: visits, bookings, enquiries or awareness?
4. What content work have you paid for before, if any? What did you actually receive?
5. What facts about the offer can be stated publicly, and who verifies them?
6. Who must approve the script, filming, music, staff/customer appearances and final post?
7. Would four reviewed scripts and a weekly calendar solve a current problem, or do you need filming/editing instead?
8. What is the smallest paid pilot you would consider? What would make it worth renewing?
9. What date would the work need to be delivered?
10. May I follow up once with a scoped quote? (Record consent privately, not in this repository.)

Do not argue with a “no,” imply the sample has proven results, or treat hypothetical interest as a sale. A paid pilot is validated only when scope is accepted and payment is actually received through an owner-selected, lawful method.

## 5. Execution checklist

- [ ] Owner chooses one price and final offer wording.
- [ ] Create a sample by clicking **Load sample** in the local app; remind the viewer it is fictional.
- [ ] Owner selects 10 warm/local prospects and contacts them personally; keep names, contact details and private notes outside Git.
- [ ] Conduct conversations and record only consented, minimum-necessary notes in the owner's private workspace.
- [ ] Quote only after confirming actual scope, delivery date, rights, revision and invoice/payment terms.
- [ ] Deliver manually; have the owner review every script and the client approve its own claims/assets.
- [ ] Ask for renewal and permission before using any outcome as a public testimonial or case study.

## 6. Decision rule after ten conversations

Treat **three paid pilots** as an initial validation target, not a forecast. For each one record privately: time to deliver, revisions, collected amount, direct costs, client satisfaction, permissioned result and renewal decision.

- **Continue / refine** if buyers pay, delivery is repeatable, and contribution after direct costs and owner time is acceptable.
- **Change the offer** if customers consistently ask for a different deliverable (for example, editing rather than scripts).
- **Stop and interview again** if people praise the idea but nobody will pay. Do not respond by building more agents or claiming hypothetical ad revenue.

`Contribution = collected payment − refunds − transaction fees − direct production/tool/contractor costs − tax reserve − owner delivery time cost.` Views and likes are not cash. No customer outreach, payment, platform payout, or revenue has been verified by this kit.
