# Entertainment — Wit & Hasya Content Model v1.0

## ENT-WIT-S1
**Wit, Wordplay, Satire & Literary Humour**

Coverage:
- wit and wordplay
- puns and riddles
- observational humour
- irony and parody
- satire and social commentary
- literary humour in prose and poetry
- stage, screen, radio and television humour
- regional-language humour
- Indian and world humour traditions
- digital humour, memes and future formats
- history and evolution of humour

## ENT-HASYA-S1
**Hasya Kavi Sammelan, Hasya Kavita & Indian/World Humour Literature**

### Historical timeline
Origins and oral traditions → early print culture → Kavi Sammelan development → radio era → television era → post-liberalisation stage/commercial era → satellite/OTT era → YouTube/social media → contemporary live events → future digital/hybrid formats.

### Kavi Sammelan
Track:
- Kavi Sammelan history
- Hasya Kavi Sammelan
- Mushaira/Kavi Sammelan relationships
- event organisers and venues
- public, cultural, educational and corporate events
- diaspora events
- regional variations
- event archives
- performance formats
- historical and contemporary recordings where rights permit

### Poet knowledge graph
For every poet, where evidence exists:
- name and pen name
- language and region
- birth/death or active period
- literary genre
- biography
- mentors/lineage where documented
- major performances
- published books/collections
- magazines/newspapers
- awards
- radio/TV/film work
- digital presence/current activity where verified
- source provenance
- rights status

### Indian humour/satire literature
Include historical and modern writers/poets such as:
- Kaka Hathrasi
- Harishankar Parsai
- Sharad Joshi
- K. P. Saxena
- Suryakumar Pandey
- Gyan Chaturvedi
- Ashok Chakradhar
- Surendra Sharma
- Arun Gemini
- Shambhu Shikhar
- and additional verified regional-language poets/writers

Names are examples for research indexing, not a closed list.

### World humour literature
Expand beyond Hindi:
- English
- Urdu
- Marathi
- Bengali
- Gujarati
- Punjabi
- Tamil
- Telugu
- Kannada
- Malayalam
- Assamese
- Odia
- other Indian languages
- major world humour/satire traditions

### Published literature
Index books, poetry collections, satire collections, magazines, newspaper columns, anthologies and archival publications. Store bibliographic metadata and rights status. Do not reproduce copyrighted works beyond lawful limits.

### Content products
- poet biographies
- literature explainers
- historical timelines
- Kavi Sammelan documentaries
- poet/genre comparison
- book/collection guides
- archival audio/video discovery
- interviews where lawfully available
- event calendars
- educational shorts
- historical vs contemporary humour series
- Indian vs world humour comparisons

### Evidence labels
VERIFIED_FACT / HISTORICAL_RECORD / CURRENT_VERIFIED_STATE / TREND / FORECAST / SCENARIO / TRADITIONAL_BELIEF / UNVERIFIED_CLAIM

### Revenue path
RESEARCH → RIGHTS CHECK → CREATE → VERIFY → OWNER PUBLISH GATE → PLATFORM → ANALYTICS → REVENUE EVENT → KUBER RECONCILIATION → AUDIT → LEARN

### Safety and rights
- no fabricated quotations
- no invented poet biographies
- distinguish humour from defamatory claims
- preserve copyright/provenance
- use public-domain or licensed material for reproduction
- respect living persons and sensitive satire
