# Vyomaraj Collaboration & Global Earning Fabric v1.0

## Objective
Make Collaboration a first-class autonomous agent that continuously discovers legitimate opportunities from creators, publishers, music channels, authors, brands, professional networks, social platforms and other AI agents, then routes each opportunity through Vyomaraj/Jarvis and Kuber.

## Operating model
INBOUND/OUTBOUND DISCOVERY -> IDENTITY CHECK -> RIGHTS CHECK -> OPPORTUNITY SCORE -> BEST AGENT MATCH -> PROPOSAL/SCOPE -> OWNER GATE -> EXECUTE -> DELIVER -> ACCEPTANCE -> INVOICE -> PAYMENT -> KUBER RECONCILE -> ROI -> LEARN.

## Content opportunity classes
- creator-to-creator collaborations
- AI-agent-to-AI-agent collaborations
- music/audio listing and licensed promotion
- book reading/narration where rights permit
- book/article/script summaries and commentary
- paid copywriting
- translation/localization
- podcast production
- social-status/channel distribution
- branded content
- affiliate/product content
- educational media licensing
- documentary/research production
- presentation/visualization production
- editing/voiceover/animation services
- research/data/content services
- sponsored series
- licensing and syndication
- consulting and production partnerships

## Global opportunity rule
"No world left for earning" means continuously scan lawful markets and emerging formats, not that every opportunity is automatically pursued. The system must score opportunity quality and reject scams, rights ambiguity, spam, policy violations and uneconomic work.

## Social tabs / surfaces
Every supported platform is represented as a capability surface. A tab is considered ENABLED only after account connection, authorization, API/integration test, publish test and analytics test. Merely having a UI tab or configuration file is NOT enabled.

## Autonomous strategy
Vyomaraj/Jarvis continuously compare:
- content demand
- audience response
- creator gaps
- platform algorithm/format changes
- monetization eligibility
- sponsor demand
- affiliate potential
- licensing opportunities
- inbound requests
- AI-agent collaboration opportunities
- production cost
- expected net revenue
- payment reliability

Then create ranked experiments, execute within policy, measure and scale winners.

## Guardrails
- No fake engagement.
- No purchased/fabricated views, likes or subscribers.
- No mass unsolicited messaging.
- No copyright infringement.
- No unauthorized book/music/voice/likeness use.
- No credential sharing with collaborators.
- No external party receives root access.
- Paid spend and binding contracts require owner approval.
- Platform terms and regional eligibility are mandatory.
- Financial commitments flow through Kuber.
