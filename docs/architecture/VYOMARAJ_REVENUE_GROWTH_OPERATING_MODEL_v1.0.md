# Vyomaraj Revenue Growth, Sales & Marketing Operating Model v1.0

Sales, marketing, growth and partnerships are a capability layer under the existing architecture. The ₹5–10 crore annual ambition is a strategic target range, not a forecast.

## Operating model

DISCOVER -> RESEARCH -> SEGMENT -> POSITION -> OFFER -> TEST -> DISTRIBUTE -> ATTRACT -> QUALIFY -> CONVERT -> DELIVER -> RETAIN -> RECONCILE -> LEARN -> SCALE.

## Capabilities

- market/customer research
- ICP and segmentation
- lead discovery and qualification
- offer and pricing design
- proposals/outreach
- partnership/sponsorship/affiliate discovery
- negotiation support
- conversion/retention
- ROI and unit economics
- controlled marketing experiments

Every experiment records audience × problem × offer × platform × format × hook × CTA and measures conversion and net revenue rather than vanity metrics.

## Technology

Tools remain replaceable capabilities beneath Harness. Use APIs for deterministic integration, MCP for approved tool/resource access, A2A for allowlisted agent collaboration, first-party analytics where available, owner-approved scopes and server-side secrets.

## Revenue truth

METRIC -> ATTRIBUTION -> REPORTED EARNINGS -> FEES/ADJUSTMENTS -> SETTLEMENT -> PAYMENT EVIDENCE -> KUBER RECONCILIATION.

No platform becomes ENABLED merely because its adapter is configured.
