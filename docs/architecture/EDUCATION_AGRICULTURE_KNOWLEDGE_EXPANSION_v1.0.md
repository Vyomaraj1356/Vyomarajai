# Education + Agriculture Knowledge Expansion — 2026-10-07

## Education

Education is the universal knowledge and civilization spine. The new sub-agent layer covers:

- **EDU-S21 — World & Indian Customs, Cultures & Traditions**
  - world regions, India, states/UTs, local/regional customs, districts, villages, communities and diaspora
  - festivals, rituals, social practices, languages, beliefs and living traditions
- **EDU-S22 — Bharat State, District & Village Knowledge**
  - state → district → city/town → village/locality
  - local history, geography, governance, economy, education, agriculture, arts, food, festivals, heritage and community knowledge
- **EDU-S23 — Constitution of India & Constitutional History**
  - Constitution, Constituent Assembly, Preamble, Articles, Schedules, rights, duties, DPSPs, institutions, amendments and constitutional evolution
- **EDU-S24 — Indian Laws, Rules, Regulations & Governance**
  - Acts, codes, rules, regulations, notifications, institutions, regulatory systems and legal/compliance literacy
- **EDU-S25 — Foreign Affairs, Diplomacy & Indian Foreign Policy**
  - foreign policy, diplomacy, treaties, bilateral/multilateral relations, neighbourhood, Indo-Pacific, trade, defence diplomacy and diaspora
- **EDU-S26 — Indian Administration & Civil Services**
  - administrative history, IAS/IPS/IFS/IFoS and other services, recruitment, roles, cadres, district administration, training, ethics, reforms and public-service case studies
- **EDU-S27 — Law & Justice: Cases, Verdicts & Legal Literacy**
  - landmark cases, facts, issues, arguments, reasoning, holdings, remedies, verdicts and practical plain-language explanations of what/when/where/who/how
- **EDU-S28 — Governance Case Studies, Solutions & Best Practices**
  - Indian and international governance examples, failures, interventions, solutions, implementation and outcomes
- **EDU-S29 — Every Indian Aspect — People, Institutions & Public Life**
  - people, communities, institutions, public services, economy, education, health, science, technology, culture, sport, agriculture, industry, infrastructure, media, law and governance
- **EDU-S30 — Local India & Living Heritage**
  - state/district/village knowledge, oral history, local heroes, crafts, folk arts/music, languages, foodways, architecture, sacred places, ecology and community memory

### Legal/current-information rule

Law, regulations, constitutional interpretation and foreign-policy current affairs are time-sensitive. The runtime must attach source, jurisdiction, effective date/version and provenance. Historical law must be distinguished from current law. Case summaries must distinguish facts, issues, arguments, judgment/reasoning and final holding; they are educational knowledge, not personalized legal advice.

## Agriculture & Environment

- **AGRI-S7 — Forestry & Forest Sciences**
- **AGRI-S8 — Fisheries, Aquaculture & Aquatic Life**
- **AGRI-S9 — Beekeeping, Bees & Pollinators**
- **AGRI-S10 — Animals, Birds, Insects & Living Creatures**

These cover origin/history → present → future, taxonomy/species, anatomy and life cycles at an educational level, habitats, ecological functions, human relationships, Indian and world biodiversity, agriculture relationships, conservation and sustainable practices.

Wildlife/animal information must respect applicable conservation, animal-welfare and environmental law. No illegal wildlife trade, poaching or harmful acquisition/use instructions.

## Universal inheritance

Every new agent inherits:

Primitive/Origin → Old → Historical Evolution → History → Present → Current State → Trends → Future → Possible Futures

Evidence labels:

VERIFIED_FACT · HISTORICAL_RECORD · CURRENT_VERIFIED_STATE · TREND · FORECAST · SCENARIO · SPECULATION · TRADITIONAL_BELIEF

Local knowledge must preserve locality, language, source/provenance and community context rather than flattening all Indian culture into one category.
